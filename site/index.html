Quaderno

A private, encrypted journal for GNOME.

Quaderno is a journal for GNOME. Journal pages, dreams and quick notes live in one encrypted file on your computer.

The goal is a journal that stays private by default. Your writing stays yours: no account, no server, no network.

For people who want to keep a journal on their own computer.
